Relink, the latest brainteaser from the Daily Mail, is not just another puzzle game; it's a daily challenge that tests your lateral thinking and problem-solving skills. With its unique format of four rows and four tiles, players must identify three words that share a common link while spotting the imposter that doesn't belong. This innovative gameplay encourages players to think outside the box and engage their minds in a fun and stimulating way.

What sets Relink apart is its clever design, where each day's puzzle is crafted to reveal a hidden connection among the imposters. This adds an extra layer of complexity that keeps players coming back for more. As you navigate through the rows, you'll find that the game rewards creativity and intuition, making it a delightful experience for puzzle enthusiasts.

With tips from the Puzzles Editor to guide you, such as starting from the top row and vocalizing the words, players can enhance their chances of solving the puzzle. As Relink goes live every day at midnight, it promises to be a refreshing addition to the Daily Mail's Games section, inviting players to challenge themselves and their friends.
