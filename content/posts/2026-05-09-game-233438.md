In a bold move to reshape the gaming landscape, Arjan Brussee, co-founder of Guerilla Games and former technical director at Epic Games, is developing 'The Immense Engine'a game engine designed to serve as a European alternative to dominant American and Chinese technologies like Unreal Engine and Unity. Brussee emphasizes the need for a fully European-hosted engine that adheres to local regulations, aiming to cater not only to gaming but also to sectors like defense and logistics that require sophisticated 3D simulations.

Brussee's vision for The Immense Engine includes groundbreaking integration of AI, which he believes can revolutionize game development. He argues that existing engines are limited by traditional design principles that prioritize mouse-driven interfaces. By leveraging AI, he envisions a system where a small team can accomplish the work typically done by a much larger group, thus streamlining the development process and enhancing creativity.

As the gaming industry continues to evolve, the introduction of a European-centric engine could provide developers with new tools and opportunities. Will The Immense Engine pave the way for a new era of game development in Europe? Only time will tell.
