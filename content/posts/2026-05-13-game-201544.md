As Zeuss intern, players are tasked with creating intricate traps and scenarios that keep Sisyphus perpetually on the brink of escape, only to be thwarted at the last moment. This unique gameplay mechanic not only challenges your strategic thinking but also tickles your funny bone with its dark humor.

The games design encourages players to think outside the box, turning each level into a delightful exercise in frustration and creativity. With each contraption you build, youre not just playing a game; youre participating in a comedic narrative that reflects on the futility of certain pursuits. As you orchestrate Sisyphus's never-ending cycle, you might find yourself reflecting on your own challenges in life. Will you be able to break the cycle, or will you too find yourself back at square one?
